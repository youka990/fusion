What Is Fusion?

Fusion is the process of joining two atomic nuclei to release enormous amounts of clean energy. This is the same reaction that powers the Sun.

Tokamak

A Tokamak is a donut-shaped magnetic fusion device that traps ultra-hot plasma using magnetic fields.

AI Control

AI can control plasma behavior by predicting instabilities and adjusting the magnetic coils in real time.
